MedCore Platform

Med Core is a specialized Arabic e-learning platform built for medical, dental, and pharmacy students. It delivers professional courses and high-quality medical content from a selected group of teachers in a clean, focused, RTL-first experience.

Students and teachers each get role-based accounts, teacher accounts are verified through an admin approval flow, and a multi-layer anti-piracy system protects course videos with traceable dynamic watermarks and device limiting.

  • Role Full-Stack Developer
  • Duration 1 month
  • Audience Medical, Dental & Pharmacy Students
  • Status Live
  • Role-based accounts for students and teachers
  • College selection (Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy)
  • Admin approval flow for teacher accounts
  • Device limiting to prevent account sharing
  • Dynamic watermark with the student's identity
  • Smooth animated, responsive RTL interface
